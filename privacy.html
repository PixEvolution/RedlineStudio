Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026 · Applies to redlinestudio.dev and everything on it.

1. Who we are

RedlineStudio (the "Service") is a browser-based game platform operated by Redline Digital, an independent game studio based in Michigan, USA ("Redline Digital", "we", "us"). Redline Digital decides what data the Service collects and why. For any privacy question or request, email REPLACE-ME@proton.me.

In short: an account needs only a username and a password. We never ask for your real name, address, phone number or birth date. Most of what you do on the Service is public by design. This policy lists exactly what is stored, who can see it, and how to remove it.

2. What we collect

2.1 Your account

  • Username. 1–10 characters, chosen by you and shown publicly. Don't use your real name.
  • Password. Sent to and stored only by Google Firebase Authentication, which stores it in hashed form. Redline Digital never receives, stores or can read your password.
  • Login identifier. Firebase Authentication needs an email-format login ID, so the Service derives one from a one-way hash of your username (…@users.redlinestudio.dev). It is not a real mailbox and never receives mail.
  • Account record. Stored in our database: your username, your Firebase user ID (uid), when the account was created, your coin balance, the date of your last daily coin claim, and whether an email is linked (yes/no only, never the address).

2.2 Email (optional, 13+ only)

If you link an email in ⚙ Account, the address is stored only in Firebase Authentication, never in our database. It becomes a second way to log in, and it's used to send verification and password-reset messages through Firebase. We don't use it for newsletters or marketing, don't show it to anyone, and don't share it. Accounts with a declared under-13 age bracket cannot link an email.

2.3 Age bracket

To unlock age-rated content you enter a birth date once in ⚙ Account. Your browser turns it into an age bracket (under 13, 13+, 16+ or 18+). Only the bracket is saved, in a private record that only your login can read. Other players can't see it. The birth date is never sent to or stored by us. Once saved, the bracket cannot be changed from the site. Accounts created before September 25, 2026 kept their bracket on the public account record; it moves to the private record automatically the next time that account visits.

2.4 Things you make and do

  • Games you publish, including their scripts, content rating, play count, and like/dislike counts. Casino machines also store a coin pool and the most recent spin.
  • Models you save, list on the Market, or buy (buying copies the model into your inventory).
  • Profile information and the custom profile page you build in the Page Studio.
  • Comments (up to 300 characters) on games, players and models; forum threads and posts (up to 1,000 characters); likes and dislikes.
  • High scores: your username, uid and personal-best score on each game that keeps scores.
  • Coin activity: daily claims, Market purchases, and Casino spins, funding and collections, recorded as changes to account balances and machine pools.

2.5 The arcade floor, live watching and multiplayer

  • Seats and the line. For each game, the database records who is seated or waiting in line (by username), when they sat down or joined, periodic "still here" timestamps, and any "wrap it up" requests or skip votes.
  • Your current machine. One record per account stores your username, uid, the game you're currently at (or none), whether you're seated or in line, and when that last changed. This enforces "one account, one machine."
  • Machine chat. Messages (up to 120 characters) are stored with your username and a timestamp. Each machine keeps its most recent 50 messages. Accounts without a declared 13+ bracket send only fixed quick-chat phrases.
  • Watch window. While you're the main player at a machine, your browser publishes images of your game screen with your username: about 5 per second to a live feed, plus a slower snapshot stored with the machine's seat record. These show only the game canvas, never your camera, desktop or other tabs.
  • Online multiplayer. In duels and multi-seat rooms, your browser exchanges game state (positions, inputs, events) with other players through Firebase Realtime Database, tagged with your uid and a timestamp.

2.6 Reports and moderation

If you use ⚑ Report, we store your username, the game reported, and your reason (up to 300 characters). Only moderators can read reports.

2.7 Technical data collected by our service providers

Redline Digital does not run its own analytics or tracking. When you use the Service, our providers automatically receive technical data that any website visit produces: your IP address, browser and device information, and request logs. They use it to deliver the Service, keep it secure, and prevent abuse (for example, Firebase Authentication rate-limits repeated failed logins). See section 5 for who they are.

2.8 Stored on your own device

The Service uses your browser's local storage, not cookies, for:

  • your logged-in username (a display shortcut; access is controlled by your Firebase login);
  • Firebase Authentication's own session data, which keeps you logged in;
  • if you've linked an email: which email belongs to which username, so username login keeps working in that browser. On a shared computer, log out and clear site data if you don't want this remembered;
  • preferences: screen size, on-screen control layouts, and collapsed Studio panels.

Advertising cookies are covered in section 6.

3. What is public

RedlineStudio is a public platform. Anyone, including visitors who aren't logged in, can see or technically retrieve:

  • your account record: username, uid, coin balance, account creation date, and whether an email is linked;
  • your published games, listed models, profile and profile page, comments, forum posts, likes, and high scores;
  • which machine you're at, seat and line activity, machine chat, and the watch-window images of your game screen;
  • multiplayer game data you send during online play.

Your password, your linked email address, your age bracket, and your reports are not public. Don't put personal information in anything you publish, chat or post.

4. How we use information

We use the information above only to:

  • run the Service: accounts, publishing, the Studio, the Market, coins, the Casino, the arcade floor, and multiplayer;
  • enforce age ratings, the Terms of Service, and database security rules;
  • send account emails (verification, password reset) to a linked email;
  • moderate content and respond to reports and legal requests;
  • keep the Service secure and prevent cheating and abuse.

We do not sell or rent personal information, do not "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising, do not build profiles of players, and do not use your content to train AI models.

5. Service providers

These companies process data for us to deliver the Service, each under its own terms and privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): Authentication (logins, passwords, linked emails), Cloud Firestore (the main database), and Realtime Database (live watching and multiplayer). The Firebase code libraries are loaded from Google's servers (gstatic.com).
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.): hosts the website's files. GitHub receives visitors' IP addresses when serving pages.
  • Google AdSense (Google LLC): advertising (section 6).

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the safety of players or the public, or to enforce our Terms. If Redline Digital's business is ever transferred, this policy would continue to apply to data collected under it.

6. Advertising

To keep the Service free, pages load Google AdSense code. Ads only ever appear at the bottom of pages, never beside or over a game. As of the effective date, ads are not yet being served.

When ads run, Redline Digital requests non-personalized ads only, for every visitor. These ads are chosen from general context such as the page, not from your browsing history. Google may still use cookies or similar technologies, and receive your IP address, to limit how often an ad is shown, report ad performance, and detect fraud. Where the law requires consent (such as in the EEA, UK or Switzerland), Google's consent message asks you first.

Learn more at how Google uses information from sites that use its services, and manage Google ad settings at adssettings.google.com.

7. Children's privacy

The Service is open to all ages and is built to collect as little as possible from children:

  • no account requires a real name, email, phone number, address or birth date;
  • age brackets are private, so no one can look up which players are children;
  • under-13 accounts cannot link an email and so have no way to be contacted off the Service;
  • typing messages other players can read (comments, forum threads and posts, and typed machine chat) requires a declared 13+ bracket. Under-13 and undeclared accounts chat at the machines only by picking from a fixed list of phrases, so they can't type personal information into chat;
  • 16+ and 18+ content and the Casino are locked for under-13 players, and for anyone who hasn't declared an age;
  • ads are non-personalized for everyone (section 6).

Children should never put personal information in usernames, games, profile pages, or anything else they publish. Moderators remove content that does. Parents and guardians: an account's footprint is what's described in this policy. You can delete your child's account with ⚙ Account → Delete (you'll need the password), or email REPLACE-ME@proton.me and we will review your child's account and delete it and its content. If we learn we've collected personal information from a child under 13 beyond what's described here, we will delete it.

8. How long we keep data, and deleting it

We keep account data until you delete it. ⚙ Account → Delete is immediate and permanent. It erases:

  • your Firebase login (username login, password, and any linked email);
  • your account record: coins and profile page;
  • your private age record and your current-machine record;
  • your published games (and their comments and scores, which become unreachable) and your models.

What remains after self-deletion unless you ask us to remove it:

  • comments you posted on other people's games, players and models, and your forum threads and posts, still showing your former username;
  • your high-score rows on other players' games;
  • machine chat messages, until pushed out by newer ones (each machine keeps its latest 50);
  • likes and dislikes you cast, and copies of models other players bought from you (those belong to their inventories);
  • reports you filed, until a moderator resolves them.

To have any of that removed too, email redlinedigitalgames@proton.me from your linked email, or tell us your username if you have none. We'll act within 30 days. A casino machine that still holds coins must be emptied before its account can be deleted.

Automatically temporary data: live watch-window frames are deleted when the player disconnects; seat and line entries expire within minutes of the last "still here" signal; the stored snapshot and multiplayer slot data are overwritten by later play. Service providers keep their own logs under their own retention policies.

9. Your rights and choices

You can view and edit most of your data on the site, and delete your account yourself at any time. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, and not to be treated differently for using these rights. To make a request, email REPLACE-ME@proton.me. Because accounts have no identity attached, we may ask you to prove you control the account, for example by emailing from its linked address or by confirming a detail only the account holder would know.

Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: we process data to provide the Service you asked for (performance of a contract), for our legitimate interests in running a safe, working platform (security, moderation, abuse prevention), to comply with legal obligations, and, for advertising where required, with your consent. You may complain to your local data protection authority. Data is processed in the United States by the providers in section 5, which rely on recognized transfer safeguards.

10. Security

Passwords are handled only by Firebase Authentication. Database security rules enforce who may change what: no player can edit another player's games, models, comments, posts or profile, and coin balances cannot be created from nothing. No system is perfectly secure. Because passwords may be as short as one character, choose a longer one and link a verified email so you can recover your account.
