🎒 Courses

Learn to make games the way games learned to exist — one machine at a time, starting in 1947, assuming you know absolutely nothing.

How courses work

Every course is one museum machine, taught in three movements: the history (what it was, why it mattered), the autopsy (our rebuild opened up, its logic explained line by line), and the build — you make your own game using what that machine taught, step by step.

A course opens in its own Studio window with the lesson docked as a panel — your normal Studio draft is never touched. And the steps are live-verified: the lesson watches the real workspace and ticks each step the moment you've actually done it. Finish every step and the course is complete — completing a course unlocks the next, in museum order, so by the end of the ladder you've rebuilt the history of game design with your own hands.

Progress saves to your account, so log in before you start. (Logged out, the course still runs — it just can't remember you finished.) Each course ends with a machine you can publish to the Games page, high-score table included.
