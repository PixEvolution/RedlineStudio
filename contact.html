Contact

Two ways to reach Redline Digital — this form (no account needed), or email RedlineDigitalGames@proton.me. Both land with the same person.

Send a message

Parent/guardian and privacy requests get an answer within 30 days; moderation appeals within 14 days. Leave a way to reply if you want one — the form works without it, but then the answer can only be an action, not a message back.



Faster routes for some things

  • A game breaking the rules or misrated: the ⚑ Report button on its page reaches moderation directly.
  • Deleting your own account: ⚙ Account → Delete is immediate — no need to write in.
  • General questions: the Forums are open.
  • Legal, privacy, copyright and security notices are valid through this form or the email above, as the Terms and Privacy Policy say.
